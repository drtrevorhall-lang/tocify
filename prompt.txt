## UP-WEIGHT
 
- Pediatric and adolescent samples. This is the core population.
- Prospective and longitudinal designs with defined follow-up intervals.
- Outcomes measured with objective neuropsychological tests rather than parent report alone.
- Normative data, validation studies, and replication.
- Multi-site or consortium samples, and registry or network studies.
- Reporting of reliable change, base rates, and clinically meaningful thresholds.
- Preregistration and open data.
- Studies addressing equity, access, or performance of measures across languages
  and cultural groups.
 
## DOWN-WEIGHT
 
- Cross-sectional single-site studies with small samples presented as definitive.
- Parent- or self-report-only outcomes with no objective cognitive measure.
- Adult-only samples with no plausible pediatric translation.
- Acute imaging or biomarker work with no cognitive or functional outcome.
- Animal models with no clear translational link.
- Case reports and small case series.
- Narrative reviews, editorials, commentaries, corrections, errata, and book reviews.
  Score these below 0.40 unless the review is a substantive methodological synthesis.
